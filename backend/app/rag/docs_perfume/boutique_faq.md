# Boutique FAQ (demo policies)

DEMO POLICIES. Everything in this document is an illustrative policy written for the ZUNO demo boutique. These are not Dior's policies, the boutique is not affiliated with Dior, and nothing here is an offer from Dior. The boutique is an independent luxury beauty boutique in Hong Kong that carries Dior fragrance, makeup and skincare. All prices are in Hong Kong dollars (HK$).

## Prices and discounts (demo policy)

Boutique prices are fixed and match the price list. Like the luxury houses we carry, the boutique does not discount fragrance, makeup or skincare for individual purchases, and there are no price matches or promo codes. Instead, every purchase comes with complimentary extras: deluxe samples, gift wrapping and, on eligible bottles, engraving. Corporate gifting orders of 10 or more pieces are handled by the boutique manager and may qualify for corporate gifting pricing of up to 5 percent.

## Complimentary samples (demo policy)

Every fragrance purchase comes with two complimentary deluxe samples, chosen with the client to suit their taste. Clients who are still deciding may take home up to three complimentary sample vials of fragrances they tried in the boutique, so they can wear a scent for a day before buying. Skincare purchases come with a complimentary sample of a matching product from the same routine.

## Complimentary engraving (demo policy)

Complimentary engraving of up to 12 characters (initials, a name or a short date) is available on eligible fragrance bottles of 100 ml and above. In the boutique it takes about 15 minutes while the client waits. For delivery orders it adds one business day. Engraved items are personalised and cannot be returned or exchanged unless faulty.

## Complimentary gift wrapping (demo policy)

Every purchase can be gift wrapped free of charge in the boutique's signature box with a ribbon and a handwritten message card. Gift receipts without prices are available on request. For several gifts in one order, each can be wrapped separately with its own card.

## Delivery across Hong Kong (demo policy)

Complimentary standard delivery across Hong Kong on orders of HK$800 or more; below that, standard delivery is HK$60. Standard delivery takes 1 to 2 business days to Hong Kong Island and Kowloon, and 2 to 3 business days to the New Territories and outlying islands. Same-day courier delivery is available on Hong Kong Island and Kowloon for HK$120 on orders confirmed before 2pm. The boutique does not ship outside Hong Kong.

## Returns and exchanges (demo policy)

Unopened products in their original sealed packaging can be returned or exchanged within 14 days with the receipt. For hygiene reasons, opened fragrance, makeup and skincare cannot be returned unless faulty. Engraved and personalised items are final sale. A faulty product is replaced or refunded in full.

## Fragrance and beauty consultations (demo policy)

The boutique offers a complimentary 30-minute in-boutique fragrance consultation with a beauty advisor: trying scents on skin, finding a signature fragrance, or choosing a gift. Makeup and skincare consultations are also complimentary and last about 30 minutes. Consultations can be booked by phone, and the advisor will have a selection ready based on what the client shared on the call.

## Corporate gifting (demo policy)

Orders of 10 or more pieces for a company, an event or client gifts are corporate gifting orders. They get a dedicated advisor, individual gift wrapping and message cards for each recipient, and a single delivery or delivery to each recipient across Hong Kong. Corporate gifting pricing of up to 5 percent may be approved by the boutique manager against a single confirmed order. Individual purchases are not eligible.

## Visiting the boutique (demo policy)

The demo boutique is in Central, Hong Kong, open 10am to 8pm every day except public holidays. Payment by Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UnionPay, Octopus, Apple Pay and Alipay HK. Testers of every fragrance on the shelf are available to try.
